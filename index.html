ABOUT JDM AEROCOATS

Specialized Finishing Facility for Critical Engineering

JDM AeroCoats is a specialized surface treatment facility providing precision finishing solutions for the aerospace, defence, automotive, and engineering industries.

We focus on controlled chemical and electrochemical surface treatment processes, supported by process monitoring, inspection, documentation, and traceability.

Our objective is to provide reliable surface protection and finishing solutions aligned with customer drawings, technical specifications, and application requirements.
